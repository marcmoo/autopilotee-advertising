# Template — DM outreach (Instagram / X / LinkedIn)

> Short, specific, human. Reference something real about the lead. One link max.

## Instagram (Turo host with active listings)

```
Hey {Name} — love the {vehicle} in your {Location} lineup. Quick one: ever
thought about running your bookings under your own brand instead of a platform
cut? We built carsharingwhitelabel.com for hosts exactly like you — your own
web + mobile app. Happy to show you: https://calendly.com/jidosoju/30min
```

## X DM

```
Saw your post on {topic}. We made a white-label rental app (P2P + traditional)
that hosts launch in weeks under their own brand. Worth a quick look?
carsharingwhitelabel.com
```

## Guardrails
- Daily cap: 15. No identical mass DMs. Stop on no-reply after frequency cap.
- Honor opt-outs immediately and permanently.
